The problem

Trading apps give you prices. AI trading agents give you claims.

  • An AI agent's track record is whatever its operator's logs say. Nobody outside can check it.
  • A market app gives people no reason to open it tomorrow, except another price chart.
  • Clock In turns one prediction a day into a habit, and writes both sides of it on chain: yours and the AI's.
How a Clock In works

One call a day. Three taps.

1

Pick one of 8 coins. Call it UP or DOWN for the next 4 hours.

2

Your wallet (Phantom or Solflare) signs one devnet transaction through Mobile Wallet Adapter.

3

Only then does SMT reveal its own call on that coin, with its reasons.

4

4 hours later both calls are graded against the real price. You get a notification.

The Solana part

Every Clock In is a memo signed by your wallet.

SMT Clock In v1 | BTC | me UP @ 85482.01 binance | SMT DOWN 36% WAIT | grade +4h

Both calls, one timestamp

Your call and SMT's call land in the same transaction, so neither can be written after the market moves.

Checkable without the app

The memo is plain text with the entry price and its source. Anyone can re-grade a call from Solana Explorer and public price data.

History lives on chain

Streak and history are rebuilt from the wallet's transactions (getSignaturesForAddress), so a reinstall or a new phone loses nothing.

Free to play

Devnet only. A Clock In costs about 0.000005 devnet SOL from the public faucet. No real money moves.

The reveal

An AI that shows its work.

  • SMT is a multi-persona trading agent: order flow, whales & on-chain, sentiment, technical, regime and catalyst each vote.
  • A judge turns the votes into a call and explains it in plain words, including why it's sitting a coin out.
  • When SMT sits out, the app grades its lean and says so. If the feed is more than 6 hours old, SMT isn't scored.

Built during the hackathon

A native Android app, written in October 2026.

  • Kotlin + Jetpack Compose. Three native tabs; SMT World's web app is one of them.
  • Mobile Wallet Adapter for connect and signAndSendTransactions.
  • The memo transaction is built by hand and matches solders (Rust solana-sdk) byte for byte. Devnet accepts it.
  • No Google Play Services. The same APK runs on Fire TV, where wallet features hide themselves.
  • Reused, already public: SMT World's web app and SMT's decisions feed. The trading agent itself isn't in the repo.
