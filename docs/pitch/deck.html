The problem

Trading apps give you prices. AI trading agents give you claims.

  • An AI agent's track record is whatever its operator's logs say. Nobody outside can check it.
  • A market app gives people no reason to open it tomorrow, except another price chart.
  • Clock In turns one prediction a day into a habit, and writes both sides of it on chain: yours and the AI's.
How a Clock In works

One call a day. Three taps.

1

Pick one of 8 coins, or SKR. Call it UP or DOWN for the next 4 hours.

2

Your wallet (Phantom or Solflare) signs one devnet transaction through Mobile Wallet Adapter.

3

Only then does SMT reveal its own call on that coin, with its reasons.

4

4 hours later the call is graded on the market price. You get a notification.

Steps 1 to 3: my phone, my ETH call of 12 Oct. Step 4: rendered from the app's v0.3.3 code with live devnet data.
The Solana part

Every Clock In is a memo signed by your wallet.

SMT Clock In v1 | ETH | me UP @ 2530.59 binance | SMT UP 40% WAIT | grade +4h

Both calls, one timestamp

Your call and SMT's land in the same transaction, so neither can be written after the market moves.

Checkable without the app

The memo is plain text with the entry price and its source. Anyone can re-grade a call from Solana Explorer and public price data.

History and leaderboard from the chain

A second memo names one shared registry address. Your history and everyone's leaderboard are read back from Solana, so there's no server to trust.

Free to play

Devnet only, paid with free SOL from the public faucet. No real money moves.

The reveal

An AI that shows its work.

  • SMT is a multi-persona trading agent: order flow, whales & on-chain, sentiment, technical, regime and catalyst each vote.
  • A judge turns the votes into a call and explains it in plain words, including why it's sitting a coin out.
  • SMT is scored only when it calls LONG or SHORT. When it sits out, the app shows its lean and doesn't grade it.

Everyone vs SMT

A leaderboard with no server.

  • Players reads every Clock In from the shared registry address and ranks players after 3 graded calls.
  • SMT gets its own row. Its call for a coin and hour is the one most players recorded, so one player can't rewrite it.
  • The SMT tab shows the agent's view of all 8 coins, and unlocks after your call for the day.

Made for a phone

SKR, a widget, and a card to share.

SKR is a ninth coin to call. SMT doesn't trade it, so it's you vs the market. The app reads your SKR balance on mainnet, read-only.

Any call can be shared as a card with its transaction.

A home-screen widget keeps the streak in view. It reads the phone's cache, so it costs no network.

Built during the hackathon

A native Android app, written October 7 to 12, 2026.

  • SMT, the trading agent, and SMT World, its web app, existed before. This Android app is new.
  • Kotlin + Jetpack Compose. Four native tabs: Clock In, My calls, Players, SMT.
  • Mobile Wallet Adapter for connect and signAndSendTransactions. The memo transaction is built by hand and matches solders byte for byte.
  • No Google Play Services. The same APK runs on Fire TV, where wallet features hide themselves.
